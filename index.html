About

Kanishka Srivastava is an engineer inspired by literature and life, blending technical reasoning with creative expression. Through his words, he crafts stories that linger long after the pages are closed.

He is the author of internationally recognized books The Adventures of Eshani and the Seasons, Ekansh and the Night of Falling Stars, and The Sargam of Silence.

His literary journey has earned recognition among Top 100 Poets of India (S7 Poetry) and Top 10 Authors of India Project (Milaan Publication). He has been honoured by Inkhub Publications for his creative excellence.

Explore more moments, writings, and milestones—and connect with him on Instagram — @superpoet_2022 .

The Adventures of Eshani and the Seasons

A Magical Bedtime Journey Through India’s Six Seasons

Guided by a vibrant butterfly, Eshani journeys through India’s six seasons, discovering their beauty, meaning, and quiet magic.

A soothing bedtime story that gently introduces children to nature, change, and wonder.

Ekansh and the Night of Falling Stars

A Magical Bedtime Journey Through Stars

One quiet night, Ekansh discovers fallen stars in his backyard and, with a cheerful firefly named Jugnu, sets out on a gentle journey to return them to the sky before sunrise.

A heartwarming bedtime story about wonder, friendship, and the quiet belief that even the smallest light can brighten the darkest night.

The Sargam of Silence

A Journey Through Friendship, Love, and Self-Discovery

In this compelling tale, a group of boys navigate the fragile years of adolescence, each quietly carving his own path through confusion, growth, and change.

A reflective story exploring introspection, empathy, and kindness hidden within the chaos of growing up.
