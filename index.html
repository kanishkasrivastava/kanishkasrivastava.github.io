About

Kanishka Srivastava is an engineer inspired by literature and life, blending technical reasoning with creative expression. Through his words, he crafts stories that linger long after the pages are closed.

He is the author of internationally recognized books including The Adventures of Eshani and the Seasons, Ekansh and the Night of Falling Stars, and The Sargam of Silence.

His literary journey has earned him recognition among the Top 100 Poets of India by S7 Poetry and a place in the Top 10 Authors of India Project by Milaan Publication.

His works have been featured in multiple national and international anthologies. His Hindi piece “Mere Papa Mere Hero” secured 3rd place in The Aurora’s Embrace Vol-3, and he has also been honored by Inkhub Publication for his creative contributions.
